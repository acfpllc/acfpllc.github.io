Who we serve

Boards, homeowners, and self-managed communities

ACFP works with homeowners associations, condominium associations, and townhome communities across Chicagoland. As agents of the Board of Directors, managers put the community’s interests first while handling day-to-day operations and communications.

Remote management is built for self-managed communities that want a clearer structure, boards that need financial oversight and administrative support, and associations that want third-party operations without the overhead of full on-site management. It is available throughout most of the United States, with a few state exceptions.

New management accounts receive a complimentary legal review of all association insurance policies, arranged with an independent attorney. ACFP does not provide legal advice. The review can cover the master property policy, general liability, directors and officers (D&O), fidelity or crime bond, umbrella, and workers’ compensation, and a plain-language summary is presented at a board meeting.

  • Homeowners associations

    Assessment billing, vendor payments, maintenance coordination, and board administration for residential HOAs.

  • Condominium associations

    Support for buildings and common elements, including financial reporting, repairs, and closing documents.

  • Townhome communities

    Management shaped around each association’s governing documents, shared areas, and budget.

  • Self-managed boards seeking help

    Remote financial oversight, owner and board support, and virtual meeting assistance without full-service pricing.

Why boards choose ACFP

A local office, not a national call center

The people who answer the phone work out of the Elgin office. ACFP describes itself as more than an HOA management company: a partner to communities, with a foundation of transparency, accountability, and customer service.

The company is women-owned and locally operated. Owners pay and pull documents through a secure online portal. The board stays in charge: managers give a professional opinion, then follow the board’s decision. Vendor contracts are reviewed for savings before renewals.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How do homeowners access their accounts or make payments?

Homeowners can find portal access links on the ACFP website to check a balance, make a payment, update information, and open documents in a secure online portal.

How long does it take to receive resale and refinance documents?

Typically, ACFP provides resale and refinance documents within 3 to 5 business days, depending on request volume and verification requirements. See resale and refinance services for what a disclosure package can include.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency support?

Yes. ACFP provides 24-hour after-hours emergency response for urgent property issues that affect safety or building structure. Call 911 for immediate threats to life or safety.

Can you help our HOA reduce costs?

ACFP analyzes current vendor contracts, operational efficiency, and maintenance strategies to identify cost-saving opportunities.

What areas do you currently serve?

ACFP manages associations in Chicago and 13 suburbs across Cook, Kane, DuPage, Will, McHenry, and Lake counties, and also works with associations in neighboring Chicago suburbs. Remote management is available in most U.S. states, with a few exceptions. See the service areas page or ACFP’s areas we serve page.
